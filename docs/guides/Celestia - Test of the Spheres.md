Test of the Spheres (Celestia's final instance; quest #94 Trial of the Spheres), from the player, 2026-10-04.

FLOOR 1 (Star)

Test of Constellation: Use each of the stands once to align the stars, completing this puzzle.

Test of the Shining Stars: Four lights are in a + formation. Use them in the order of: Bottom, Left, Top, Right, lighting them all to complete the puzzle.

Test of Vision: Turn all four heads to face the bowl to complete this puzzle.

1: Astraeus - Rank 11 Boss, Astral, 7210 health, starts with 6 power pips.
On the first or second turn, he will say "The Constellations Form Again!", put up a 90% tower shield, and call three minions. The 90% tower cannot be removed until all the minions are defeated, except by using Pierce or Steal Ward. The minions will only use attacks such as 1-pip Natural Attack, Leprechaun, Troll and Fire Elf, but they will frequently Sprite the boss. When they are all dead, the tower shield can be removed. This cycle repeats every few turns, making for a very long battle.
Observed spells: Tower Shield, Amplify, Fortify, Power Nova, Judgement, Elemental Blade, Elemental Trap.

FLOOR 2 (Moon)

Puzzle: Use each of the stands until it locks up to align the moon holograms. Then, battle each of the four sets of minions. When each set is defeated, they will leave behind a silver chest. Get a Moon Shield from each one and give them to the statues near Ptolemos. This will open the boss's door, completing the puzzle.

2: Ptolemos - Rank 11 Boss, Lunar, 7320 health, starts with 6 power pips.
On the first turn, he will put up a 90% tower shield. It cannot be removed, however, if you or a teammate use a blade or Guiding Light, he will say "You Have Charmed Your Way Into My Heart!" and he will remove any and all shields on him, excluding stun shields. When someone attacks and does not have any blades left on after the attack, he will say "Your Charm Has Worn Thin!" and replace the shield after the attack.
Observed spells: Colossus, Frostbite, Frost Giant, Natural Attack (Storm, 125 per pip), Tower Shield, Moon Blade (+35% to next Death, Myth and Ice spells), Moon Shield (-50% to next Death, Myth and Ice spells), Wraith, Scarecrow, Elemental Shield.

FLOOR 3 (Sun puzzle)

Puzzle: There are four mirrors around the room. Each one produces a gold chest when used. Get the swords from the chests and give them to the statues to complete this puzzle and unlock the teleporter to the final boss.

FLOOR 4 (Sun boss)

3: Mithraya - Solar, Rank 11 Boss, 7100 health, starts with 6 power pips.
Whenever someone uses a spell successfully, she will interrupt after their turn and place a Smoke Mantle on them. Sometimes, after everyone has taken their turn, she will interrupt and use Smokescreen. She will only stop her mantling/screening when your blades and traps are set, under which conditions she will say "The Cards Align! Now's Your Chance!"
Observed spells: Smokescreen, Smoke Mantle, Natural Attack (100 damage per pip, Fire), Kraken, Triton, Wild Bolt, Darkwind, Sun Shield (-50% to next Life, Fire and Storm spells), Sun Blade (+35% to next Life, Fire and Storm spells), Guiding Light, Regenerate.
