Celestia side quests: how-tos from the player (2026-10-04).

Senses Working Overtime (from Edith Benchley, Celestia Base Camp) - reaching the Protector Statues in The Lunarium:
1. Go through the active Lunarium Portal in Celestia Base Camp into The Portico (the connecting instance).
2. Defeat the required Stellar Protectors and collect the Sensorium. Defeat paired protectors properly, or a barrier wall blocks the way.
3. Go further into The Lunarium instance, where the big Celestial Protector Statues stand.
4. Present the Sensorium at the statues that house the Lexicary Celestia, then return to Edith Benchley.

Press Pass: Zodiac Tomes spread over five boss areas (Optio Agenor's camp in Survey Camp, Selwyn's Chamber, Coral Castle, the Pinnacles, the Crystal Reactor): skipped.
