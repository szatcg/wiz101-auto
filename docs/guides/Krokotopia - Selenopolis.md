# Selenopolis boss cheats (the player's notes, 2026-11-03)

- **Ra (Rank 23)**: "Night must be equal to Day, which must match Night." Match his hanging
  effects (blades/traps) with as many of your own before hitting. Hitting unmatched, or any DoT,
  brings a 1,500 Balance hit and removes your charms and wards. Killing him unmatched: he revives
  (Availing Hands) and wipes your buffs. *Bot: no hit while his blades outnumber ours; no DoTs.*
- **Taking Tree**: swaps a helpful charm/ward/HoT away from its receiver; a harmful charm/ward/DoT
  on him back onto the caster. Multi-target spells only swap with the caster; Aegis/Indemnity
  protect. *Bot: no single blades, traps or shields.*
- **The Prophet & Enki** (Tomb of the Forgotten): the Prophet's shadow aura turns healing into
  damage; Enki throws DoTs, off-school blades and enemy HoTs, and on turn 5 detonates every
  DoT/HoT on the team. Don't heal; set up fast, AoE them before turn 5. *Bot: no heals.*
- **Drake Lockley & Quentin Quail**: any damage, pierce, charm, trap, aura or global brings a
  500 Storm "Duck Savage" hit on the caster (each blade one more). Avoid traps and pierces; hit
  directly, Drake first. *Bot: no traps or pierces.*
- **Anubis**: an aura shield worn down by repeated hits; after it breaks he can revive or glitch
  his health. Potent Feint as the shield breaks, then a big finisher.
- **The fourth Krokosphinx** (portal atop the central building, optional): riddles; no power
  pips while they're up; each right answer is a 100% trap on the boss.
