Luska Charmbeak cheats
- Casts Cleanse Ward to remove a Trap placed on him (pet spells don't trigger it; only the first trap of a multi-trap spell; Indemnity prevents it; AoE traps like Windstorm don't trigger it).
- "Only attack if you have been inked!": each round he Inks the wizard in the first spot (Sun) with a Smoke Mantle; other wizards get a Feint (no +30% trap on him). At 75% health (12,690 left) he also inks the second wizard (Eye spot); at 50% (8,460 left) the third (Star). Never the fourth (Moon).
- "If you can still see me, don't look at me!": a non-inked wizard casting a single-target spell at Luska (attacks, traps, negative charms, auras) makes him remove all wards from himself and all wizards, then cast Skeletal Dragon (1,200 Death) or Power Link (2,100 Fire + 600 heal over 3 rounds) at that wizard. Inked wizards can cast single-target spells at him without retaliation (he still removes traps). Anyone can cast all-enemy attacks without triggering it.
  Solo: we are in the first spot, so inked every round -> single-target hits are allowed; traps on him get cleansed (blades and AoE are fine).

Sylster Glowstorm cheats
Cycles of 4 rounds (the first light cycle 3), from round 1, repeating until all enemies are dead.
- Light cycle (first): "Traps go undetected in the light!": light Doom and Gloom; cast traps and prisms now. He removes positive charms (Cleanse Charm, 3 positive charms from the caster) if a charm is cast (blades, negative charms like Weakness/Smoke Screen, dispels; Shadow Creature blades too; pet spells don't). Dissipate dispels the cheat; Aegis keeps one charm. From round 8, at the start of this cycle: removes 3 positive charms from all wizards and 3 traps from himself and minions.
- Dark cycle (second): "The blade goes unseen in the dark!": dark Doom and Gloom; cast blades and other positive charms now. He removes one trap or prism if one is cast (Cleanse Ward); Elemental/Spirit Trap lose only the last; multi-target traps and mass prisms don't trigger it; Indemnity keeps one; Vaporize dispels it. At the start of the cycle: removes 3 traps from himself and minions.
- "No Traps?!" / "No Blades?!": a single-target attack without using a trap (light cycle) or a blade (dark cycle) makes the target cheat-cast: Sylster: Ra (1,460-1,540 Balance; Unbalance dispels); Spellhammer Sorcerer: Scarecrow (600 Death); Water-Wing: Rebirth (750 heal, 375 under Doom and Gloom). All-enemy attacks don't trigger it; single-target spells that also hit all (Sun Serpent) do.
- A global cast by a wizard is replaced with the current Doom and Gloom. Strangle dispels either Doom and Gloom (charms/traps are still removed if cast in the wrong cycle).
- "Not da' Boss!": with Sylster dead the Spellhammer Sorcerer runs the cycles (from a fresh 3-round light cycle); with both dead, the Water-Wing.
  Solo plan: AoE (Orthrus) never triggers the retaliation; traps in light rounds (1-3, 8-11, ...), blades in dark rounds (4-7, 12-15, ...); single-target hits only with a blade/trap of the right cycle on.
